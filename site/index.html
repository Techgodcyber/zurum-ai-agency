Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need any technical or design skills?

None at all. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can build a website with Zurum. Just describe what you want in your own words.

How long does it take?

Minutes. Describe your site, Zurum builds it and sends you a live preview to review — then you publish whenever you're happy.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. When you publish, Zurum connects your own domain (like yourname.com) — hosting and setup are handled for you.

Can I make changes later?

Absolutely. Just message Zurum with what you'd like changed and it updates your live site for you — no dashboards to learn.

How much does it cost?

Building and previewing is free. You pay a simple yearly fee to publish (hosting + domain included), and top up with credits only when you make edits. No monthly subscription.
